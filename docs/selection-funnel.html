How the top 7 are chosen

From every article we read in a week to the seven per category worth your time. Week 45: 2,268 articles in, 28 out.

  1. 2,197

    Filter

    Drop obvious ads and press releases

    Fixed rules on links and titles: "/sponsored/", "Advertorial", PR wires

  2. 2,052

    Group

    Merge articles about the same story

    Text similarity finds near-identical coverage; more outlets means more weight

  3. 1,600

    Triage AI

    Label every article by topic and importance

    A fast model reads each headline and summary: category, type, importance 1–5, ad risk

  4. 100

    Shortlist

    Keep the best 25 per category

    Ranked by importance and type; one per story, capped per source, no PR or podcasts

  5. 100

    Read

    Fetch each full article

    Downloads the page, spots paywalls and hidden "sponsored by" notes

  6. 100

    Judge AI

    Score quality, catch hidden ads

    A strong model rates significance, substance, insight, writing and independence

  7. 28

    Pick AI

    An editor chooses 7 per category

    The strong model ranks them; rules enforce variety and keep ads out of the top 5
