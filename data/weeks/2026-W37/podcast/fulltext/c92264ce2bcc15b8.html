Dr Martens hires ex-Burberry VP as global customer director

Dr Martens has named former Burberry global vice president for online to offline, Katie Morton, as its global customer director following a raft of recent product and brand leadership appointments.

By Jade Burke 11 September 2026

Comment

Joining the leadership team, Morton will take up her new role on 26 October where she will report to chief brand officer, Carla Murphy.

She will be responsible for strengthening the footwear brand’s consumer-centric approach globally, alongside ensuring more consumers discover, connect with and deepen their relationship with the brand.

Morton will spend time strengthening the consumer-first operating model and reinforce the role of consumer insight and experience in building brand relevance, loyalty and long-term growth.

Before joining Dr Martens, Morton spent 17 years at Burberry across roles including director of development – digital commerce and ecommerce manager, as well as her most recent position as global vice president for online to offline.

For the last 10 months, she has worked as an independent consultant, advising brands including Fortnum & Mason on consumer strategy, transformation and international growth.

Advertisement

Murphy said: “Katie has an outstanding track record helping some of the world’s most iconic brands better understand and serve their consumers. She brings a powerful combination of consumer insight, commercial expertise and deep experience leading transformation at scale, alongside a proven track record of building high-performing teams and capabilities that deliver meaningful business impact.

“This appointment is another important step in enhancing the capabilities that support our levers for growth strategy. Together with the recent appointments across our product and brand leadership teams, Katie’s arrival strengthens our ability to better understand our consumers, respond to their evolving needs and create memorable brand experiences that deepen our connection with our consumers around the world. We’re thrilled to welcome her to the team.”

Morton’s appointment comes as the brand named Isadora Versiani as global product design director and Nadia Svensson as global merchandising and product director, earlier this month.
